# Deep Research

## Workflow

1. Restate the research question and decision it informs.
2. Break it into a few sub-questions.
3. Prefer official docs, primary repositories, release notes, and authoritative references.
4. Compare agreement, disagreement, and missing evidence.
5. Deliver a concise recommendation with confidence and limitations.

## Output

- Summary
- Findings by sub-question
- Recommendation
- Confidence
- Sources

## Guardrails

- Separate facts from inference.
- Flag single-source claims explicitly.
- Prefer recency when the topic is fast-moving.
